👋 Welcome to Kcal Tracker!

Here is a quick guide to help you get started with tracking your daily calories effectively:

1. Set Up Your Daily Goal

Go to the Profile tab, enter your height, weight, age, and activity level to compute your recommended daily calorie deficit.

2. Log Daily Meals

Use the Log tab to record what you eat throughout the day. Enter food names and calorie counts manually.

3. Search USDA Database

Use the Food Guide tab to look up exact official nutrition data and auto-calculate calories based on gram weight.

4. Track Progress

Check the Summary tab anytime to see your daily progress and intake history.
